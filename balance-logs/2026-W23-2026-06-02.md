## Follow-up Balance Adjustment: 月鏡 Unit Drive

- User feedback: `月鏡・満月の照王` had too many drive materials for its effect, and unit drive cards should not all sit at printed charge cost 6.
- Adjustment:
  - Printed charge cost changed from 6 to 5.
  - Drive materials changed from `月鏡` unit 2 + any `月鏡` 2 to `月鏡` unit 1 + any `月鏡` 2.
- Balance intent: keep the bounce/search/team ATK role intact, but make this a lighter unit drive than the heavier 6-cost finishers. The card now trades a smaller printed charge cost and 3-material requirement for a moderate finisher effect rather than asking for 4 materials.
