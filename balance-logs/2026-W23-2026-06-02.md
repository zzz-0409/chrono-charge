### Balance Evaluation

- `双彩` was already strong before this run, so the new cards avoid a direct damage burst, broad removal, or universal draw/charge activity.
- `双彩のキュー係カホ` smooths access to existing `双彩` spells but costs a hand card when the pair engine is not active.
- `双彩ライトキュー` is defensive and better before the pair condition is complete, preventing the card from simply amplifying the deck's strongest board states.
- `汎用照準補正` is a low-impact generic filter with a comeback clause gated by lower LP and an opponent unit.
- The 144-game smoke sample put `双彩` at 59.4%, below the pre-change 72-game 87.5% sample, so no immediate nerf or further buff was made.
- `星導` remains the top smoke performer and `黒機`/`電脳` remain low, but those adjustments are left for later rotation-scoped runs.
